VQEG's validation tests are made possible only by generous contributions made by the **Independent Lab Group (ILG).**  The ILG act as independent arbitrators. Their goal is to ensure that all VQEG validation testing is unbiased and done to high quality standards. The fees that VQEG charges for model validation are used to partially defray ILG costs. Most of the ILG work is donated by companies and organizations on a volunteer basis.

If you are interested in joining the ILG, please contact the <a href="https://vqeg.org/%7BlocalLink:9f794038-398c-4833-afcb-6e4e48ef67cc%7D" type="Document" title="Contacts">VQEG Co-Chairs</a> and also the ILG Co-Chair, <a href="mailto:mpinson@ntia.doc.gov" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Margaret Pinson</a>.
