## Organization links

The following contains some of the organizations that have contributed to VQEG activities. These websites may contain information, tools, and products. [Contact VQEG](https://vqeg.org/%7BlocalLink:9f794038-398c-4833-afcb-6e4e48ef67cc%7D "Contacts") to have your website inserted below.

- [AccepTV](http://www.acceptv.com) - provides solutions and service to measure perceptual video quality.

- [AGH University of Science and Technology Multimedia Team](http://qoe.kt.agh.edu.pl/) (publications, projects, etc).

- [AGH University of Science and Technology Video Quality Resources](http://vq.kt.agh.edu.pl//) (video quality research, metrics, paper references).

- [AVCN - University of West of Scotland](http://www.uws.ac.uk/home/)

- <a href="https://aveq.info/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">AVEQ</a> - Video quality and network performance measurement solutions

- Cardiff University: the Visual Attention and Visual Quality Toolbox (<a href="https://sites.google.com/site/vaqatoolbox/" rel="noopener" target="_blank" title="VAQA-Toolbox">VAQA-Toolbox</a>), has computational saliency evaluations and eye tracking data.

- <a href="http://www.ip-label.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">ip-label</a> - End to end monitoring and supervision solutions for quadruple play services and critical applications.

- <a href="http://www.itu.ch" rel="noopener" target="_blank">ITU (International Telecommunication Union) web site.</a>

- <a href="http://www.kwillcorporation.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">K-WILL Corporation</a> - Manufactures video quality monitoring and measurement equipment for the broadcast industry. 

- Netflix

- <a href="http://www.netscout.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Netscout</a>

- <a href="https://vqeg.org/n3/video" rel="noopener" target="_blank">NTIA/ITS Video Quality Research</a> - research papers and royalty free software tools for video quality estimation.

- <a href="http://www.pevq.org" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Opticom</a> - provides OEM modules as well as end user software for the assessment of voice, audio and video quality.

- <a href="http://www.pixelmetrix.com" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Pixelmetrix</a>

- <a href="http://www.qualinet.eu/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Qualinet</a> European network on Quality of Experience in multimedia systems and services.
